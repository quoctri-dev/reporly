# Reporly — Competitive Landscape
> Last updated: 2026-03-24

## Positioning Statement
> "Julius phân tích giỏi nhưng output xấu. ChatGPT thông minh nhưng không export đẹp.
> Reporly = AI hiểu data + OUTPUT CHUYÊN NGHIỆP (PDF/PPTX) sẵn sàng present."

## Direct Competitors

| Product | Pricing | Strengths | Weaknesses | Our Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Julius AI | Free / $20-37/mo | NL query, file-first, charts | Output = browser only, no PDF export đẹp | Export quality + templates |
| Powerdrill | Free / ~$15/mo | Budget, Excel/CSV focus | Basic reports, limited customization | Professional templates |
| ChatGPT + Code Interpreter | $20/mo (Plus) | Powerful analysis, flexible | No professional export, no templates, manual | 1-click report, consistent format |
| DashThis | $49+/mo | Marketing dashboards, white-label | Marketing only, expensive, no AI analysis | General purpose + cheaper |
| Relevance AI | Workflow-based | CSV report workflows, customizable | Complex setup, developer-focused | Simple UX, non-dev friendly |

## Indirect Competitors
- **Power BI / Tableau**: Enterprise, expensive, complex → we target SMBs
- **Google Looker Studio**: Free but manual setup → we auto-generate
- **Canva Docs**: Beautiful output but no data analysis → we do both
